Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Short version: the contact form, and nothing else. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no third-party trackers.

The contact form

When you send an enquiry we receive your name, company, email address, the discipline you selected, and your message. We use it to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. That is the only purpose.

The form is handled by our own function running on Vercel, which passes the message to Resend to deliver it as email. Neither service is given the data for its own use. The resulting email lives in our inbox.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers. The only cookies this site could set are none, which is why there's no cookie banner.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your details with anyone.
  • We don't add you to a mailing list. If we reply, it's a person writing back.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become work are deleted within 12 months. If we end up working together, the correspondence is kept for as long as the engagement runs plus the period our accountant requires afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, and we'll do it. If you're in the UK or EU, those are rights you have under the UK GDPR and GDPR respectively; we'd do the same regardless of where you're writing from.

Email peakoperationspartner@gmail.com and say what you'd like. No form to fill in.

Who we are

Peak Operations Partner, Cebu City, Philippines. Questions about any of this go to the same address.
