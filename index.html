What We Do

Commercial refrigeration and cold storage in Cebu

Hwasung Refrigeration has been building and servicing commercial cooling systems since 1996. From our base in Mandaue City we design, install, maintain and rehabilitate cold storage for businesses across Cebu and the wider Visayas — restaurants, grocers, cold-storage warehouses and property managers who cannot afford a system going down.

  • Cold Storage Design & Installation

    Complete cold rooms designed around the product you store and the temperature you need to hold, then installed and commissioned on site.

  • Walk-In Chillers & Freezers

    Chiller and freezer rooms for kitchens, groceries and storage facilities, built to keep stock at a steady temperature through a full working day.

  • Blast Freezers

    Rapid freezing for operations that need product brought down to temperature quickly rather than over several hours.

  • Preventive Maintenance & Repair

    Scheduled servicing and repair work that catches problems while they are still small, instead of waiting for a room to fail.

  • Check-Up & Inspection

    An engineer looks over an existing installation and reports on what is working, what is close to failing, and what it will take to fix.

  • Upgrades & Rehabilitation

    Older cold rooms brought back up to standard — panels, controls, condensing units and evaporators replaced or upgraded in place.

Equipment is sourced from established Korean manufacturers, including Samyoung and Kyung Dong Industrial, alongside Bitzer compressors. See the full service list or get in touch to talk through a project.
