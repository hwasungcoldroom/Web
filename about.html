Our History

Three decades of cold storage.

Hwasung Refrigeration began in Korea in 1996 as 화성냉동공사 — a workshop building and servicing cold rooms, chillers, and industrial refrigeration for local businesses.

The same trade, and the same standards, carried over when the work moved to the Philippines. Thirty years on we are still doing what that first shop did: designing, installing, and keeping cold storage running.

About Hwasung

Built by refrigeration engineers, run like a service company.

Hwasung Refrigeration started as a two-person repair crew and grew into a full-service commercial refrigeration and HVAC contractor. We work with restaurants, grocers, cold-storage warehouses, and property managers who can’t afford a system going down.

Every technician we send out is factory-trained, background-checked, and equipped to diagnose the problem on the first visit — not the third.
