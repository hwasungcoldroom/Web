How the bench works

01

One named operator

Every engagement is assigned to a specific person, by name, matched to the discipline you need. Not a pool, not a ticket queue.

02

A founding-team member behind them

Someone on the founding team scopes the work, sets the standard it's delivered to, and is on the hook if it slips. You can always escalate past your operator.

03

The bench absorbs the rest

Holiday, illness, a discipline outside your operator's depth: cover comes from inside Peak, already briefed. Your engagement doesn't stop.
