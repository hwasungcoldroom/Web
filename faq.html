FAQ

Common questions

What are your service hours?
Our office and service teams operate Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM. If a system fails, call or message us as early in the day as you can and we will do our best to get a technician to you the same day.
What areas do you service?
We're based in Mandaue City and serve Cebu-wide. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location.
Do you work on both refrigeration and HVAC systems?
Yes — our technicians are cross-trained on commercial refrigeration and HVAC, so one team can handle your whole building.
How does the free consultation work?
Submit your name and phone number through our booking form. A member of our team will call you directly to discuss your needs and, if useful, schedule an on-site visit — at no cost.
Do you offer maintenance contracts?
Yes. We offer scheduled preventive maintenance plans tailored to the size and criticality of your equipment.
