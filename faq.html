FAQ

Refrigeration & cold storage FAQ

What products do you offer?

We specialize in supplying and installing:

  • Walk-in Freezers
  • Walk-in Chillers
  • Blast Freezers

Our refrigeration systems are designed for commercial and industrial applications, providing reliable temperature control and energy-efficient performance.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes. Our experienced technicians handle professional installation to ensure your refrigeration system operates safely and efficiently.

Do you offer maintenance and repair services?

Yes. We provide preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair services to help maximize equipment performance and reduce unexpected downtime.

How often should refrigeration equipment be maintained?

We recommend preventive maintenance at least every six months, while high-usage facilities may benefit from quarterly servicing to maintain efficiency and extend equipment life.

Do you provide after-sales support?

Yes. Our commitment doesn’t end after installation. We offer technical support, maintenance, and repair services to keep your refrigeration system operating efficiently.

Why choose Hwasung Refrigeration Company Inc.?

Nationwide delivery and installation across Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao, customized refrigeration solutions, a professional installation team, and fast technical support at competitive pricing — trusted by commercial and industrial businesses.
